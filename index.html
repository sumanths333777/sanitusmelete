Explain Photoelectric Effect
✨ Core Idea
Photoelectric effect is the emission of electrons from a metal surface when light of suitable frequency falls on it.
📌 Key Observations
• Threshold frequency exists

• Electron emission is instantaneous

• Kinetic energy increases with frequency
💡 MEBI Insight

Higher frequency means higher energy photons.
🎯 NEET Shortcut

f > f₀ → emission occurs
⚡ Exam Tip

Einstein equation is very important for NEET numericals.
